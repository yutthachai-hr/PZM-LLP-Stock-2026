## Changes

- Click or tap a row/card to open the existing OrderSheet. Enter and Space work on focused rows. Nested checkboxes, links, buttons, menus and selected text do not open the sheet.
- One primary status badge. Supplier confirmation stays visible, even when overdue, partial receipt or resend takes priority. LINE send status never implies supplier acceptance. Pending date proposals explicitly remain pending. Legacy received orders with missing/short quantities are not called fully received.
- One filled next-step action and the existing secondary menu. Receive and receive-remaining remain directly accessible. Edit, send, cancel and close-short keep their original eligibility predicates. Risky commands still open ReasonModal.
- Supplier, PO, location and item count share one column. Tablet order date joins metadata. Due dates omit repeated prefixes. Filters stay available; date window, Excel and PDF remain grouped.
- Shared DataTable changes are additive safe activation. Shared RowMenu gains focus, arrow/Home/End/Escape support and 44px touch targets.
- `needsResend` was relocated unchanged to pure `src/lib/poRevision.ts`, and re-exported from its original service. This avoids importing browser/backend initialization into a pure presentation function. No order operation, rule or data shape was changed.
